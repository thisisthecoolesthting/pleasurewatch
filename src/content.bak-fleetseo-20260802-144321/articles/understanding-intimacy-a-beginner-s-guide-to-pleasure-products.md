## Materials Matter: Safety First  

The material of a pleasure product affects safety, durability, and ease of cleaning. Look for:  
- **Medical-grade silicone**: Non-porous, hypoallergenic, and easy to clean.  
- **ABS plastic**: Rigid, non-porous, and body-safe.  
- **Stainless steel or glass**: Smooth, hygienic, and temperature-responsive.  

Avoid porous materials like jelly rubber or TPE/TPR, as they can harbor bacteria. Always check for phthalate-free labels.  

## What to Look for When Choosing a Pleasure Product  

Selecting the right product depends on personal preferences, experience level, and intended use. Consider:  

### 1. **Purpose**  
- Are you looking for external or internal stimulation?  
- Do you want vibration, texture, or pressure?  

### 2. **Size & Shape**  
- Beginners may prefer smaller, smoother designs.  
- Curved or angled toys can target specific pleasure zones.  

### 3. **Power & Controls**  
- Adjustable speeds and patterns offer versatility.  
- Some prefer simple one-button controls, while others enjoy app connectivity.  

### 4. **Ease of Cleaning**  
- Non-porous materials are safest and easiest to sanitize.  
- Some toys are waterproof, making cleaning simpler.  

### 5. **Noise Level**  
- Quieter motors are ideal for discretion.  

## Caring for Your Pleasure Products  

Proper maintenance ensures longevity and hygiene:  
- **Clean before and after use** with warm water and mild soap or a dedicated toy cleaner.  
- **Store properly** in a cool, dry place—some materials degrade when touching others.  
- **Check batteries or charging ports** to avoid corrosion.

## What to Look for in Couples Toys with Shared Control Features  

### Material Safety  
Always choose toys made from body-safe materials like silicone, ABS plastic, or stainless steel. These materials are non-porous, hypoallergenic, and easy to clean, reducing the risk of irritation or infection.  

### App Functionality  
Consider the app’s features and ease of use. Look for apps that offer customizable vibration patterns, long-distance control, and real-time synchronization. Ensure the app is compatible with your smartphone’s operating system.  

### Connectivity  
Check the toy’s connectivity options, whether it’s Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or another method. Reliable connectivity is essential for seamless operation, especially for long-distance couples.  

### Comfort and Design  
Choose toys with ergonomic designs that are comfortable to wear or use during extended periods. Flexible or adjustable designs can enhance comfort and fit.  

### Waterproofing  
Waterproof toys are easier to clean and maintain, making them a practical choice for couples. They also allow for use in the shower or bath, adding versatility to your play.

## The 8 Best Delay Sprays & Creams for Prolonged Intimacy (Ingredient Focus)  

For many individuals and couples, premature ejaculation can be a frustrating challenge. Delay sprays and creams offer a practical, non-invasive solution by temporarily reducing penile sensitivity, helping prolong intimacy. However, not all products are created equal—ingredients, safety, and ease of use matter. Below, we review eight of the best delay sprays and creams, with a focus on their key ingredients and how they work.  

### What to Look for in a Delay Spray or Cream  

Before choosing a product, consider these factors:  
- **Active Ingredients**: Look for clinically tested compounds like lidocaine or benzocaine (topical anesthetics) or natural alternatives like clove or peppermint oil.  
- **Safety**: Avoid products with harsh chemicals, parabens, or excessive alcohol, which can cause irritation.  
- **Absorption Time**: Faster-absorbing creams (5–10 minutes) are more convenient than sprays requiring longer wait times.  
- **Numbing Intensity**: Some products offer adjustable numbing (e.g., mild vs. strong formulations).  
- **Partner Safety**: Opt for body-safe, non-transferable formulas if engaging in oral sex afterward.

## Safety Tips for Using Delay Sprays & Creams  
- **Patch Test**: Apply a small amount to your forearm first to check for irritation.  
- **Avoid Overuse**: Excessive application can cause discomfort or temporary erection difficulties.  
- **Clean Before Intercourse**: Wipe off excess product if your partner is sensitive to residual numbing agents.  
- **Consult a Doctor**: If you have skin conditions or allergies, seek medical advice before use.  

Delay products can enhance intimacy when used responsibly. Whether you prefer a fast-acting spray or a natural cream, choosing the right formula depends on your sensitivity levels and ingredient preferences. Always prioritize body-safe, well-reviewed options for the best experience.

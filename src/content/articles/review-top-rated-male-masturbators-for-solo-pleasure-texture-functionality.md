### What to Look for in a Male Masturbator  

- **Material Safety:** Opt for body-safe, non-porous materials like medical-grade silicone or phthalate-free TPE. Avoid jelly rubber, which can harbor bacteria.  
- **Cleaning & Maintenance:** Enclosed designs should disassemble easily. Look for dishwasher-safe parts or quick-drying materials.  
- **Texture Variety:** Ribs, spirals, or nodules can intensify sensations—choose based on sensitivity preferences.  
- **Size Compatibility:** Check internal dimensions if girth or length is a concern. Some sleeves are stretchable, while others have fixed sizes.  
- **Discretion & Storage:** Smaller sleeves are portable; larger automated toys may need dedicated storage.  

### Hygiene & Care Tips  

- Clean with warm water and mild soap or toy cleaner after each use.  
- Allow thorough drying to prevent mold (microfiber towels help).  
- Store in a cool, dry place; some brands include ventilated cases.  
- Replace if material becomes sticky, discolored, or torn.

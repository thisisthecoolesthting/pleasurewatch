### What to Look for in a Remote Control Vibrator  

When selecting a remote control vibrator for couples, consider the following:  

- **Connectivity:** App-controlled models offer more versatility, while physical remotes are straightforward.  
- **Fit & Comfort:** Look for ergonomic shapes that stay in place during movement.  
- **Noise Level:** Quieter motors are essential for public or discreet play.  
- **Material Safety:** Medical-grade silicone is non-porous and body-safe.  
- **Battery Life:** Rechargeable models are more cost-effective than disposable batteries.  

### Safety & Care Tips  

- Always clean vibrators before and after use with toy-safe cleaners.  
- Use water-based lubricants to avoid damaging silicone surfaces.  
- Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.  
- Check for app permissions if using Bluetooth-enabled devices for privacy.
